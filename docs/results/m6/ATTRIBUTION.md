# M6 evidence attribution and reuse

- Dryad inventory/metadata: Hanlan, J.; Dillavou, S.; Durian, D.; Liu, A. (2025),
  *Cornerstones are the key stones*, DOI https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.cvdncjtb5,
  CC0 1.0 as specified by the dataset API. No raw MATLAB/text measurements were
  retrieved or redistributed. The cited article/preprint has a separate license.
- Delft acquisition metadata and its derived CSV/plot content: Fransen, M. P.;
  Schott, D. L.; Langelaar, M., seven 100mm-outlet datasets linked from
  https://doi.org/10.4121/C.6415736, each CC BY-SA 4.0. The metadata-derived
  portions of `file_inventory.csv`, `acquisition_metadata.csv`,
  `acquisition_uncertainty.png` and compatibility summaries are provided under
  [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  Changes: selected metadata fields, standardized audit columns, conflict flags
  and a new plot; raw images/text and calibration archives are not redistributed.
- Experimental native-profile figure: Yogi, J.; Khakhar, D. (2026),
  https://zenodo.org/records/20554446, CC BY 4.0. `native_profile_limits.png`
  visualizes only directly parseable experimental y/uy pairs; no string repair,
  unit assignment or physical agreement is claimed. Simulation panel is our own.
- Simulation/numerical figures reuse our frozen M2/M3 evidence. Conceptual
  diagrams are our assessment, not measured sensitivity/calibration results.

No external license is asserted to replace the repository's source-code license.
Raw artifacts remain in ignored local storage; their URLs and checksums are
listed in `provenance_manifest.json` and `file_inventory.csv`.
